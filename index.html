Core capabilities

Built around the proposal modules: authentication, inventory, requests, allocation, and audit.

01

Role-based access

Admin, Staff, Lab Technician, and Hospital Requester each see the workflows they need.

02

Compatibility engine

Filters available, unexpired, lab-cleared units, then ranks exact match, expiry, and scarcity.

03

Alerts & dashboards

Low-stock and near-expiry thresholds keep scarce groups visible before they are wasted.

04

Audit trail

Every donation, clearance, request, and confirmed allocation is recorded with a timestamp.

Who uses the system

S

Blood bank staff

Record donations, manage inventory, confirm ranked allocations, and update request status.

L

Laboratory technician

Test quarantined units and mark them cleared before they can become available for issue.

H

Hospital requester

Submit requests with blood group, quantity, and urgency (Routine, Urgent, Critical).

A

Administrator

Manage users and roles, configure alert thresholds, and review reports and wastage.
